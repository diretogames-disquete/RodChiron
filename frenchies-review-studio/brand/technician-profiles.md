# Technician Profiles

Archetypes and notes that shape how the model frames a named technician. The
structured roster lives in `technicians.json` (that's what the app reads for the
dropdown and for archetype/notes); this file is a place to keep longer,
human-readable context. **Do not invent archetypes** — leave blank until you know.

## Archetypes (what they mean)

- **Quality Anchor** — the meticulous, detail-obsessed tech. Frame around care,
  precision, and consistency ("meticulous by nature").
- **First-Visit Converter** — great at turning nervous first-timers into regulars.
  Frame around ease, welcome, and "you'll want to come back."
