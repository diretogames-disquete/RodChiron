# Your Reviews, Answered in Your Voice — The Owner's Guide

*A plain-language guide to setting up and getting the most out of Frenchies Review
Studio. No tech background needed. The companion
[`INSTALL-AND-USE.md`](./INSTALL-AND-USE.md) has the technical details if you (or a
helper) want them.*

---

## Why this matters (the 2-minute pitch)

Here's the thing most salons miss: **when you reply to a review, you're not really
writing to that one client. You're writing to the next 50 people who read it before
they decide whether to book.**

Your review section is a sales page you don't control — but your *replies* are the
part you *do* control. A warm, specific, human reply says "real people work here,
they pay attention, and they care." A copy-pasted "Thank you for your feedback!"
says the opposite — it reads like a robot, and everyone can tell.

The problem: writing a genuinely good, different reply to every review takes time
and energy you don't have, especially after a full day on the floor. So most owners
either skip it or paste the same line every time.

**This tool fixes that.** You paste in a review, and in a few seconds it gives you
**2–4 genuinely different reply options written in your salon's voice** — each with
a one-line note on *why* that angle works — so you just pick the one you like, tweak
a word if you want, and post it. It's the difference between a blank page and a
short, easy decision.

What makes it different from the generic "AI reply" tools:

- **It sounds like *you*, not a robot.** The voice, your salon's facts, and your
  technicians are all built in. It opens by reacting to what the reviewer actually
  said — never "Thank you for your feedback."
- **It gives you a real choice.** Two to four different *strategies*, not the same
  sentence reworded. Picking one feels like a decision, not a rubber stamp.
- **It protects you.** Nasty reviews, legal/BBB threats, or anything about an injury
  get flagged **"needs your sign-off"** so you slow down and handle them carefully —
  it never fires off a confident public reply to a landmine.
- **It never makes things up.** No invented prices, no fake enthusiasm, no promises
  you didn't make.
- **You control the cost.** Replies cost roughly a penny each (often less), and you
  choose which AI it uses.

Nothing is ever posted automatically. You always pick and can edit first.

## What you'll need

- A computer (Mac or Windows).
- About 10 minutes to set it up once.
- **One "API key."** Think of it like a prepaid arcade card for the AI: you get a
  key from an AI company, and each reply spends a tiny bit of credit (about a penny
  or less). You'll add a card/credits with that company. We'll walk through it.

## Setting it up (pick the easy path that fits you)

### Path A — The double-click app (recommended)

1. **Install Node.js once.** Go to **nodejs.org**, download the big green **"LTS"**
   button, and run the installer (just keep clicking Next/Install). You only do this
   once, ever.
2. **Get the app folder** onto your computer (whoever sent you this can help, or use
   the download link they gave you).
3. **Double-click the launcher:**
   - Mac: **`start.command`** *(the first time, if your Mac says it's blocked:
     right-click the file → **Open** → **Open**.)*
   - Windows: **`start.bat`**
4. A little window opens and your browser pops up at the app. **Leave that little
   window open** while you use it; close it when you're done.

### Path B — The single file (no install at all)

1. Get the file **`standalone.html`** onto your computer.
2. **Double-click it.** It opens in your browser. That's it — no installing anything.

*(Small note for Path B: it works best with Anthropic, Google, or OpenRouter keys.
If you use an OpenAI key and it won't connect, use Path A instead, or switch to one
of those providers — the app will tell you if there's a problem.)*

### Getting your AI key (both paths)

Click **"API keys"** (top-right of the app). Pick one provider to start — any of
these work well:

- **Anthropic (Claude)** — great writing quality. Sign up at **console.anthropic.com**,
  add a little credit, and create a key.
- **Google (Gemini)** — very cheap. Get a free key at **aistudio.google.com/app/apikey**.
- **OpenRouter** — one key that reaches many models, good for keeping costs low. Get
  one at **openrouter.ai/keys** (choose the "OpenAI-compatible" row in the app and
  paste `https://openrouter.ai/api/v1` as the base URL).

Paste the key into the app, click **Save**, make sure that provider is selected
(the dot), and close the window. You're ready.

> **Is my key safe?** Yes. In the double-click app, your key stays on your own
> computer and is never shown back to you (only the last 4 digits). In the single
> file, it's saved in your own browser on your own device. Just don't email the
> file to other people with your key saved in it.

## Your first reply (60 seconds)

1. **Paste the review** into the big box.
2. **Click the stars** to match the rating.
3. If the review names one of your techs, pick them from **Technician** (or type the
   name).
4. Click **Generate responses.**
5. You'll get a few options. Read the little **"Why this angle"** under each.
6. **Pick your favorite.** Tweak a word or two right there if you want it to sound
   even more like you.
7. Click **Copy**, go to Google/Yelp/Facebook, and **paste** it as your reply.

That's the whole loop. After a couple of tries it takes under a minute per review.

## Getting the most out of it (maximum benefit)

These are the habits that turn "replying to reviews" into real marketing:

1. **Reply to *every* review, and do it fast.** Aim for within a day or two. Fresh
   replies signal an attentive business. This tool removes the "I don't know what to
   write" excuse.

2. **Reply to the bad ones *calmly* — that's where the real win is.** A graceful,
   non-defensive reply to a 2-star review impresses the people reading it far more
   than ten 5-star replies. Remember: you're writing for the next 50 readers, not to
   win the argument. The tool is built to do exactly this — and it'll flag the truly
   nasty or legal ones so you pause and handle them right.

3. **Name your technicians.** When a reviewer names Rheanna or Amber, the reply
   celebrates them by name. It builds each tech a following, shows you're a real
   team, and makes staff feel seen. (Add or edit your roster in **Settings ▸ Salon &
   voice**.)

4. **Keep your price current.** In **Settings ▸ Salon & voice**, set your real
   price. When someone grumbles about cost, the tool can gently frame the *value*
   (natural-nail care, real sterilization, single-use files) — never argue the
   number, just explain why the care is worth it.

5. **"Save as exemplar" the replies you love.** Whenever a reply comes out perfect
   (or you tweak it just right), hit **Save as exemplar.** The tool learns from those
   and gets more "you" over time.

6. **Turn raves into marketing.** For standout reviews, one of the options is a
   "testimonial invitation" — a gracious way to ask permission to feature their
   words. Great reviews are free advertising once you have the OK to use them.

7. **Follow the colored approval chip.** Every reply now comes with a small chip
   that tells you who should look at it before it posts, straight from your
   response playbook:
   - **Auto** (green) — anyone trained on the voice can post it after a read.
   - **Check** (amber) — have a second pair of eyes look first.
   - **Owner** (red) — you read and approve it personally. All negatives land here.
   - **Legal** (purple) — you *plus* counsel or franchise support. Injury and
     discrimination claims land here. No exceptions, no same-hour posting.
   The app enforces this: red- and purple-chip replies always carry the
   **"needs your sign-off"** banner and are marked as drafts.

8. **Read the "Deliberately left out" box.** Under the replies, the tool lists what
   it *chose not to say* — no refund talk, no arguing the timeline, no naming the
   tech in a complaint — and the risk each omission avoids. That's your playbook
   working. If you edit a reply, don't add those things back in. And when a
   **"fragile wording"** warning appears (it will on injury claims), it tells you
   which passage not to trim — edit it longer, never shorter.

9. **Keep costs tiny.** Look at the little line above the replies — it shows about
   how much each generation cost. For everyday replies, use a cheap model (pick it in
   **"Run this with"**); save the premium one for the tricky, high-stakes reviews.

## A simple weekly routine (about 10 minutes)

1. Open the app (double-click the launcher, or the file).
2. Open Google/Yelp/Facebook and find reviews you haven't answered.
3. For each: paste it in → set stars → pick the tech → **Generate** → pick → tweak →
   **Copy** → paste as your reply.
4. Any red-flagged ones: set aside and handle carefully (or with help).
5. Done. Ten minutes a week keeps your review section looking alive and human.

## Do's and don'ts

**Do**
- Read every reply before you post it — make it sound like *you*.
- Answer the negatives; they're your biggest opportunity.
- Keep your price and technician list up to date in Settings.
- Save the replies you love so the voice keeps improving.

**Don't**
- Don't post without reading (it never posts for you — that's on purpose).
- Don't argue with an unhappy client in public; own what's fair and take specifics
  to a private message.
- Don't paste a client's private personal details into the box.
- Don't share the single-file version with your key saved in it.

## Common questions

**Does it post replies for me?** No — never. You always copy and paste it yourself,
so you're in full control.

**What does it cost to run?** Roughly a penny per reply on the top-quality AI, and a
fraction of a cent on the budget ones. You pay the AI company directly; the app
itself is free.

**Where does my key/data go?** Your key stays on your device. The reviews you paste
are sent to the AI you chose (just like typing into any AI tool) to write the reply.

**What if a review names a technician I don't have?** The tool notices and quietly
asks if you want to add that name to your roster — it won't invent a person.

**Can my front-desk person use it?** Yes. It's simple enough for anyone on your team
to run the weekly routine above. Just make sure they read replies before posting,
especially the flagged ones.

**Can I move my setup to another computer?** In the single-file version, use
**Settings ▸ Salon & voice ▸ Export voice library** to save your settings and
favorite replies, then **Import** them on the other computer. (Your key isn't
included — you'll re-enter that once.)
